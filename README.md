Built specifically for the real-world **Y Combinator cross-border venture capital corporate structure**:
- **US Parent Company**: `Blackbird Finance Inc.` (Delaware C-Corp, EIN: 93-8472910)

## 🏛️ The Correct Corporate & Banking Sequence

```
1. Delaware C-Corp Formation  ➔  2. Mercury/Brex US Account  ➔  3. $500k YC Investment Inward  ➔  4. Treasury Idle Yield & India Remittance
(Blackbird Finance Inc.)          (Choice Financial / Sweep)      (SAFE Agreement Cleared)          (Cost-Plus MSA / FIRA / CA Signoff)
```

1. **Step 1: Set up the foreign parent company**
   - Incorporated as a Delaware C-Corporation (`Blackbird Finance Inc.`). Y Combinator requires investing into a US Delaware parent company via standard Post-Money SAFE agreements.
2. **Step 2: Open the startup-focused bank account**
   - USD account opened with **Mercury / Brex** (partnered with Choice Financial Group / Evolve Bank & Trust, offering multi-bank sweep coverage up to $5,000,000 FDIC insurance).
3. **Step 3: Receive YC $500k Investment Funds**
   - Standard deal wired into the US account: **$125,000 for 7%** + **$375,000 MFN SAFE** = **$500,000 USD total**.
4. **Step 4: Treasury / Savings Product for Idle Cash**
   - Idle cash is not left sitting at 0% interest. **$450,000** is placed in Mercury Treasury (Vanguard Treasury Money Market Fund, VUSXX) earning **5.02% APY**, generating ~$1,882/month in passive treasury income while remaining liquid.
5. **Step 5: Money reaches the Indian operating company**
   - **Route A: Service Fees (Recommended)**: Master Services Agreement (MSA) on a Cost-Plus (10-15%) transfer pricing model. The Indian company issues monthly export invoices for software development services and receives funds with automatic **e-FIRA** generated for GST zero-rating.
   - **Route B: Foreign Direct Investment (FDI)**: US Parent subscribes to equity in the Indian subsidiary. Requires filing Form FC-GPR via the RBI FIRMS portal within 30 days along with a Chartered Accountant (CA) valuation certificate.
   - ⚠️ *Always executed with a licensed Chartered Accountant (CA) and cross-border structuring lawyer.*
